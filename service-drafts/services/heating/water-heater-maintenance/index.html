Signs service is overdue

What the water heater is showing, and when to book a service

Most water heaters show wear long before they leak. Open the card that matches what the hot water is doing. Each one lists the usual causes, the safe checks a homeowner can make, and what a sound hot water heater service visit looks at. A gas smell or a CO alarm always comes first.

Rumbling, popping or crackling from the tank Book soon One service visit

Likely causes

  • Sediment layer on the tank bottom, boiling under the burner
  • Mineral scale on an electric lower element
  • Years without a flush in hard-water conditions

Check safely first

  • Listen during a long hot shower, when the burner or element runs longest
  • Look at the drain valve for crusty white deposits

What the technician does

A proper flush drains the tank through the drain valve until the water runs clear. The technician judges how much sediment came out, because a heavy layer means the flush interval should be shorter. On an electric tank, the lower element is checked for scale. A tank that still rumbles after a full flush may have hardened scale that no flush removes.

Rusty, brown or cloudy hot water Book soon Within a few days

Likely causes

  • Anode rod used up, so the tank steel starts to corrode
  • Rust from old galvanized piping, not the tank
  • Stirred-up sediment after a long idle period

Check safely first

  • Run cold water only: rust in the cold too points to the pipes
  • Run hot water from a tub faucet into a white bucket and compare it with cold water

What the technician does

The technician pulls and inspects the anode rod. A rod worn down to its steel core wire has stopped protecting the tank. Replacing it in time is a low-cost way to add years to a tank. If the water from the tank itself is rusty and the rod is long gone, the inner lining may already be corroding.

Likely causes

  • Temperature and pressure relief valve opening on high pressure
  • Thermal expansion with no room to go in a closed plumbing system
  • A leaking drain valve, or a leak from the tank itself

Check safely first

  • Put a bucket under the discharge pipe and dry the floor to find the source
  • Close the cold water valve above the tank if water keeps pooling

What the technician does

The technician finds where the water comes from before anything else. A relief valve that drips can point to high pressure or thermal expansion, and that cause gets fixed, not ignored. A drain valve can be replaced. A leak from the tank body cannot be serviced, and the tank needs replacing.

  • •Find the label on the side of the tank. The model and serial numbers tell the technician the age and the parts.

Service or replace?

When maintenance stops paying off, and when a tank is past service

Maintenance slows wear, but no flush undoes a corroded tank. Put a written repair price beside a written replacement price, and compare the tank's age with the lifespans below. A tank near or past the top of its range that needs a significant repair is a replacement candidate. A leak from the tank body means replacement at any age. A younger tank with a worn anode and some sediment is the tank regular water heater service protects.

Where Chicago water heaters sit, and what each setup needs

Gas tank water heaters

The most common Chicago setup. Hot water tank service here means a flush, an anode check and a draft test.

Tanks left alone on the chimney

A high-efficiency furnace like this one vents through plastic pipe. The water heater may then vent alone up the old chimney, so its draft needs a closer look.

Shared utility closets in condos

Closets that hold the furnace and water heater need free combustion air and clearance, often through a louvered door.

Combi boilers with on-demand hot water

Less common in Chicago, a combi boiler heats the home and the tap water. Its hot water side scales up like a tankless unit.

Tanks beside a boiler

In older Chicago homes with boiler heat, a gas tank often stands beside the boiler and vents into the same chimney, so the draft check covers both.

Water heater service questions, answered

What does an annual water heater service include?

Many visits are a quick drain and go: the tank is flushed, and the anode rod and relief valve are never looked at. That leaves the two parts that most affect a tank's lifespan unchecked. A full annual water heater service flushes the sediment, inspects the anode rod and lifts the temperature and pressure relief valve. Gas units get a burner cleaning and a draft test, and electric units get element checks. City & Suburban technicians are NATE-certified, and the price goes in writing before any work. How often should a Chicago home book a water heater flush service?

Most tank makers call for a flush about once a year, and the manual for the tank sets the exact interval. Lake Michigan water carries dissolved minerals that settle out as sediment when heated. The amount that drains out at one visit is a practical guide: heavy sediment calls for a shorter interval. Tankless units are descaled on a similar yearly schedule. City & Suburban maintenance plans add seasonal check-ups and priority dispatch, which keeps the interval from slipping. What does the anode rod do, and when does it need replacing?

The anode rod is a magnesium or aluminum rod that corrodes inside the tank so the steel does not. A drain-and-go flush never pulls it, so a rod worn to its steel core wire goes unseen until the water turns rusty. By then the tank lining may already be corroding. The rod should be pulled and inspected at each service, and replaced only when it is worn. City & Suburban repairs first where repair is right, and the price is in writing before any part goes in. Noah W. wrote in a Jul 2026 Google review: "They addressed our needs, answered our questions, and didn't try to upsell us."

Why does the temperature and pressure relief valve need testing?

The temperature and pressure relief valve, often called the T&P valve, opens if the tank gets too hot or the pressure climbs too high. It stops a failed control from turning the tank into a hazard. Minerals can seize a valve shut over the years. The technician lifts the test lever, confirms that water flows and checks that the valve reseats. The discharge pipe should run down toward the floor. A valve that drips after the test is replaced, and City & Suburban puts that price in writing first.

Is thermal expansion checked during a tank service?

It should. Heated water expands, and in a home with a check valve or pressure reducing valve, the extra volume has nowhere to go. The usual response to a dripping relief valve is a new valve, which leaves the pressure problem in place. A thorough service measures the water pressure. Where an expansion tank is fitted, the technician checks its air charge and whether it has waterlogged. A waterlogged tank is a common cause of a relief valve that keeps dripping. City & Suburban's pricing promise is repair first where repair is right, with the price in writing before work starts.

What does gas water heater service check?

Gas water heater service focuses on burning and venting. A typical visit cleans the burner and stops there. What gets missed is the vent. When a furnace moves to plastic venting, the water heater can be left venting alone up an old masonry chimney that is too large for good draft. A thorough visit runs a draft test at the draft hood with the burner on. It also inspects the flue baffle, the gas connection and the vent joints. City & Suburban repairs and replaces gas tank water heaters, power-vent and direct-vent models included.

What does a tankless water heater maintenance service involve?

Many tankless units run untouched until the display shows scale codes or the water swings between hot and cold. By then minerals coat the heat exchanger, and the unit works harder for less hot water. Descaling needs isolation valves on the water lines, and a unit that lacks isolation valves needs a pair added first. The service closes those valves, pumps a descaling solution through the heat exchanger and flushes it clean. The inlet water filter screen, air intake, vent and condensate drain are checked too. City & Suburban repairs and replaces tankless gas water heaters and repairs all makes and models.

Do tankless warranties expect yearly descaling?

Tankless warranties often exclude damage caused by scale, and some makers set descaling intervals for hard-water areas. The manual and warranty card for the unit give the exact terms. That makes a written record of each descaling worth keeping: the date, the work done and the error code history. A homeowner with those records has an easier conversation if the heat exchanger ever fails. City & Suburban technicians are NATE-certified, and the price for the descaling goes in writing before any work starts.

What does electric water heater service include?

An electric water heater has no burner or vent, so electric water heater service looks at the heating elements and wiring instead. With the power off at the breaker, each element and its temperature control are tested with a meter. Wire connections are checked for heat damage. The tank still needs the same flush, anode inspection and relief valve test as a gas tank. City & Suburban repairs and replaces electric tank water heaters, and repairs all makes and models.

What can a homeowner safely do between water heater services?

Some care is safe for any homeowner. Keep about 3 feet clear around the water heater. Look for drips at the fittings and the base each month, and watch the vent connector for rust or loose joints. A setting near 120°F limits scald risk and slows scale. Test the CO alarms in the home on a schedule. The gas control, burner, vent, anode and wiring are jobs for a technician. Is it risky to flush a water heater that has never been flushed?

It can be. The usual approach drains a neglected tank hard. An old plastic drain valve can then clog with sediment or fail to close, leaving a drip on the floor. Hardened sediment may not come out at all. A careful first service checks the drain valve and the age of the tank before opening anything. When does a water heater need repair or replacement instead of maintenance?

Maintenance keeps a working water heater healthy, but it does not fix a failed part. No hot water, a pilot that will not stay lit or a dead element calls for water heater repair. A tank that leaks from its body, or an old tank with heavy rust, calls for water heater replacement. For a replacement, a sound quote names the permit the job needs and who pulls it.
