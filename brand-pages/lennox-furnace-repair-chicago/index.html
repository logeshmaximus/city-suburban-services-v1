Repair & no-heat calls
Most faults are fixed on the first trip when the part is on our truck.
Lennox furnace repair near me, any hour
Call at 2 a.m. or on a Sunday. A no-heat call gets the next open technician, on any Lennox model.
SureLight ignitor & flame sensor
We test the ignitor and flame signal, then replace or clean the part that stops the burners.
Inducer & pressure switch faults
When the furnace hums but never lights, we check the inducer, switch, tubing and vent in order.
Board, gas valve & blower testing
We measure before we replace. These parts cost the most, so a meter reading comes first.
02
Maintenance & cleaning
A fall visit that catches the small faults before a cold night does.
Lennox furnace maintenance visit
Burners, ignitor, flame sensor, filter, gas pressure, drain and safety controls, written up for you.
Flame sensor & burner clean
Removes the coating that makes a Lennox furnace light and then drop out seconds later.
Blower wheel clean & airflow check
A dirty wheel starves the heat exchanger of air. We clean it and set the blower speed again.
Condensate trap & vent clearing
On 90%+ models we flush the trap, clear the drain and check both plastic pipes outside.
03
Safety & Lennox parts
Tests that protect your household, and parts that match your Lennox.
Heat exchanger camera & CO test
We look inside with a camera and measure carbon monoxide. Any crack is photographed and shown to you.
Lennox parts & warranty paperwork
We look up coverage by serial number, so you know before the visit ends what Lennox pays for.
iComfort thermostat set-up
We pair, update or replace the iComfort thermostat so it reads and runs the furnace correctly.
Combustion & gas pressure check
Gas pressure and flue gas measured on an analyzer, so the burners run clean at rated input.
04
Replace & upgrade
For when fixing it again costs more than it is worth.
Lennox furnace installation
We size the furnace to your home, fit it with our own crew and register it with Lennox for you.
Lennox furnace replacement quote
Repair cost, replacement cost and unit age on one page. You choose; we don't push.
Humidifier & air filter add-ons
Whole-house humidifiers and media filters fitted to the Lennox cabinet for dry winter air.
AC coil & cooling match-up
When the AC goes too, we match the coil and controls to your Lennox furnace's blower.
