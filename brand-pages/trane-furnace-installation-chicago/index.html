Three Trane builds, and what each means for the install
Efficiency is in the name: 80 or 95
80%
Flue to the chimney
Exhaust rises through a metal flue into the masonry chimney. We size the vent connector and check if the chimney needs a liner.
95%+
Condensing, PVC vented
Two plastic pipes leave through a wall, plus a drain. We route them away from snow drifts and windows and fit a trap that won't freeze.
XC
Communicating
Furnace and thermostat share one data link. We wire and set them up as a single system, then test every stage before we leave.

Installation & replacement

No-heat & repair calls

Trane furnace repair near me, 24/7
A real person answers at any hour. No-heat homes get the first open technician.
Ignitor & flame sensor repair
When the furnace clicks but won't light, we test the silicon nitride ignitor and flame circuit.
Inducer & pressure switch repair
We find why the draft motor or switch stops the start-up, including a blocked vent or drain.
Control board & blower motor
Checked with a meter against the fault the board reports, then replaced only if it failed.
03
Maintenance & safety
Yearly checks that keep a new or older Trane running right.
Trane furnace maintenance
Burners, ignitor, sensor, filter, inducer, drain and limits, checked and written on your report.
Heat exchanger & CO testing
A camera look inside and a CO reading in the flue and the room air, with photos of any issue.
Combustion analysis
Gas pressure and flue readings taken on an analyzer, so the burners fire at the rated input.
Drain, trap & vent clearing
For condensing Trane furnaces: frozen drains, full traps and iced wall pipes cleared.
04
Add-ons & system match

Thermostat matched to the furnace
A communicating thermostat for XC models, or a two-stage one for XV, set up with the furnace.
Humidifier & air cleaner
Whole-house humidifiers and media air cleaners mounted on the new cabinet for winter comfort.
Matching AC or heat pump coil
An indoor coil and outdoor unit paired with the new Trane blower for summer.
Duct and return air fixes
Extra return air or duct repairs when the old system was starved for air.
