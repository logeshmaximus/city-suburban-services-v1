Bradford White water heater maintenance
Flush, anode, relief valve, gas control, venting and flame arrestor checks on one yearly visit.
Sediment flush

Anode rod inspection
Pulled, shown to you and replaced if it's spent, so the tank lining stays protected.
AeroTherm filter & drain care

Flame arrestor & thermal switch

Relief valve testing

Leak and drain valve repair
We trace the water to a fitting, valve or the tank itself, and tell you which before any work.
Draft & carbon monoxide test
Draft and CO readings on every gas tank we touch, with photos if something is wrong.
