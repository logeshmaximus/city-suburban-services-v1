Condensing (RTGH, IKONIC)
A second heat exchanger makes acidic water. We clear the condensate line, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent.
Non-cond.
Non-condensing (RTG)
Hotter exhaust through a stainless vent. We check the vent seals and terminal, and descale the single heat exchanger.

A pump keeps hot water near the taps. We test the pump, the schedule and the return line on every visit.

WHAT WE DO · 16 SERVICES IN 4 GROUPS
Rheem tankless water heater service, start to finish

04
01
Fault codes & repairs
We read the code first, then test the part it points to before we replace it.
Fault code diagnosis
We read the code on the display, check the history and test the part behind it on our meter.
Ignition & flame rod repair
For units that click, try to light and lock out. Igniters and flame rods carried on the van.
Flow sensor & inlet filter
For a unit that won't fire at a low tap flow. We clean the screen and test the flow sensor.
PC board & gas valve
Both cost real money, so we confirm the fault with readings before we order the part.
02
Descaling & upkeep
Chicago's lake water leaves scale in the heat exchanger. A yearly flush clears it.
Rheem tankless water heater maintenance
Descale, inlet filter, condensate and vent checks, combustion test and code history, once a year.
Heat exchanger descaling flush
A pump and descaling solution run through the isolation valves until the scale is out.
Isolation valve kit fitting
Units installed without service valves get a kit, so every flush after that is quicker.
Condensate neutralizer refill
For RTGH and IKONIC units: new neutralizer media and a clear drain line to the floor drain.
03
Venting, gas & safety
A tankless burns a lot of gas at once. We check that it gets enough and vents it safely.
Gas line sizing check
We measure the gas pressure at full fire, the cause of many units that run cold at two taps.
Vent & termination inspection
PVC or stainless, we check every joint, the slope and the wall cap for blockage and leaks.
Combustion & CO test
A combustion reading at high and low fire, with photos if anything is off.
LeakGuard & freeze checks
We test the leak shut-off where fitted and check freeze protection on outdoor units.
04
Install & upgrade
A new tankless unit, a swap from a tank, or a recirculation add-on.
Rheem tankless water heater installation
Sized to your fixtures, gas line and venting checked, City of Chicago permit pulled for you.
Tank-to-tankless conversion
We remove the old tank, run the vent, upsize the gas line where needed and mount the new unit.
Recirculation add-on
A pump and return line, or an RTG-R or IKONIC SR with the pump built in.
Repair or replace, in writing
We weigh the age, the heat exchanger and the repair price, and put both options on paper.
