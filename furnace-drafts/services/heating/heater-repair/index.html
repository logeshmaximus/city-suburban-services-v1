Likely causes

  • Thermostat in the wrong mode, or dead batteries
  • Tripped breaker, or the furnace service switch turned off

Check safely first

  • Set the thermostat to Heat and a few degrees above room temperature

What the technician does

The visit starts with the fault code on the control board. The technician then follows the start sequence: thermostat call, inducer, pressure switch, igniter, gas valve and flame sensing. Repair or replace?

The $5,000 rule, and where a repair stops paying off

Multiply the furnace's age by the repair price. A result over $5,000 means a replacement price deserves a look beside the repair quote; under it, furnace repair usually wins. The rule only starts the conversation. A newer furnace with one failed igniter is worth fixing almost every time. A confirmed cracked heat exchanger keeps the furnace off, and on most older furnaces replacement is then the sensible call. What happens when a gas furnace will not light?

A gas furnace lights in a fixed order. The thermostat calls for heat, the inducer starts and the pressure switch proves the draft. Then the igniter heats, the gas valve opens and the flame sensor proves the flame. A failure at any step stops the burners. A sound diagnosis reads the board's fault code, then tests each step in turn, so the household pays for one repair, not a guess. City & Suburban diagnoses before quoting. Why does a furnace start, then shut off after a few minutes?

Short cycling usually means a safety control is doing its job. A dirty flame sensor loses the flame signal, so the board shuts the gas within seconds. An overheating furnace trips the high limit, often because of a clogged filter or closed vents. After a Chicago snowstorm or drifting wind, snow or ice can block the two PVC pipes on the outside wall of a high-efficiency furnace. The furnace then locks out until the pipes are cleared. A sound diagnosis measures flame signal, temperature rise and airflow to find which safety tripped. City & Suburban diagnoses before quoting, so the written price covers the cause, not only the sensor.

Can heat exchanger repair fix a cracked furnace?

Most cracked heat exchangers cannot be safely patched. A crack can let combustion gases, including carbon monoxide, mix with the air the blower sends through the home. Manufacturers treat a confirmed crack as a reason to keep the furnace off. Some furnaces carry long heat exchanger warranties, so the part itself may be replaceable. A sound inspection confirms a crack before calling one and shows the homeowner the evidence. City & Suburban recommends a furnace replacement only when the evidence supports it, and the furnace stays off until the fault is corrected.

How is electric furnace repair different from gas?

An electric furnace has no burner, flame or flue. Heat comes from resistance elements that switch on in stages through sequencers or relays. Common faults are a burned-out element, a failed sequencer, a tripped limit or a breaker that trips under load. Gas furnace repair, by contrast, centers on ignition, gas pressure and venting. Testing elements and circuits safely means isolating the power first, because an electric furnace draws heavy current. The furnace itself makes no carbon monoxide. What does the Chicago Heat Ordinance mean for a broken furnace in a rental?

Where a Chicago landlord supplies the heat, the Heat Ordinance (Municipal Code 13-196-410) generally requires at least 68°F from 8:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. and 66°F overnight. The rule runs from September 15 to June 1, and the code lists exceptions. A broken furnace in a rental is therefore a pressing repair for the owner.
